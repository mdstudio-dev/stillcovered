Privacy Policy — StillCovered

Last updated: 24 November 2026

StillCovered ("the app") is made by Ouayat Studio (contact: ouayatstudio@gmail.com). This policy explains what happens with information when you use the app.

What you save in the app

You can save purchased items with their name, category, store, price, serial number, notes, purchase date, warranty length and photos (for example a receipt or warranty card). This information:

  • is stored only on your phone, in the app's private storage,
  • is not sent to us and not shared with anyone by the app,
  • may be included in your own Android device backup if you have backups turned on.

You can delete any item (and its photos) in the app at any time, or delete everything by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage.

Photos and camera

Photos are added through Android's system Photo Picker or your camera app. The app does not ask for access to your whole gallery or storage. Only the photos you choose are copied into the app.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app shows warranty reminders. Reminders are created on your phone; no data is sent for this.

Sharing

If you tap Share, the app prepares the item's details and photos and opens Android's share menu. Nothing is sent unless you choose an app and send it yourself.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob (a banner and, occasionally, a full-screen ad after you save an item). Google may collect and process data such as your device's Advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device and app information, ad interactions and diagnostic data to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud.

  • Where required by law (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and in some US states), the app asks for your consent through Google's consent tool (User Messaging Platform) before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options.
  • You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your Android settings.
  • How Google uses data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Children

The app is intended for adults (18+) and is not directed at children.

Security

All network connections made by the app (for ads) use encryption (HTTPS).

Your rights

We hold no personal data about you on our servers. For data processed by Google for ads, use the controls above or contact Google. For any question, email ouayatstudio@gmail.com.

Changes

We may update this policy. The new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
